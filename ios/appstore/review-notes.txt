ABOUT THE APP
Party is a free app made by friends in Switzerland (our group "Vendredi Party" lives inside it): organise events, play party games. French-speaking countries only, 18+, interface in French.

SIGN-IN
Phone number + 6-digit SMS code, no password. Demo account "Apple-test" in the Sign-in information fields (fixed code). Tab "Se connecter" > "Se connecter" > number > "Recevoir un code par SMS" > code.

NATIVE FEATURES (4.2)
- Native tab bar; SwiftUI screens: Music, Settings, Groups, "Ton avis" (feedback), event AI assistant, "Pot commun" (shared expenses), announcement banner, offline screen.
- Push notifications, one switch per type; Live Activity "Ton titre arrive".
- Apple Wallet tickets, camera QR scanner, VisionKit receipt scanner, Calendar (date polls), optional Contacts (only hashed numbers compared), dictation (Speech), share extension, quick action, universal links, haptics.

WEB CONTENT (2.5.2)
The Events and Games tabs are our own website (party.bobycharbon.ch) in a WKWebView: the same HTML/JS for every user, calling only the native features above. No code is downloaded to add or change native features. Other sites open in Safari.

EVENTS (web, Events tab)
- Create an event (form or "Avec l'IA"), then "Qui est invité": who sees it (public, invitation, group), then invite.
- Invitation links, one per person or "Plusieurs" (several people, valid until the day after). Every guest can create, copy and stop own links and sees guest counts only (never names).
- Not public: someone coming through a guest's link waits for the organiser ("À accepter").
- Date poll: guests vote dates, the organiser fixes one.
- "Ajouter à mon agenda": a personal calendar feed (subscribed once) follows the event and the answers.
- Carpool (organiser's option): participants offer or take seats. No money.
- Groups: "Inviter avec un lien" (reusable group link).
- Invited people sign in like everyone (SMS code, first name, last name, nickname) before answering.
- Admins only: "Nouveau compte" notification; an announcement or maintenance message for everyone.

PAYMENTS (3.1.3(e))
Party sells nothing: no in-app purchase, no digital goods, no commission. An organiser may price a real-world event (seat, meal); participants pay the organiser outside the app (Twint, bank transfer, PayPal/Revolut link), then tap "J'ai payé". "Pot commun" only records who owes what.

AI
"Avec l'IA", writing help in "Ton avis" and receipt reading in "Pot commun" send text/images to Anthropic (Claude) only after a consent sheet ("J'accepte"); without AI everything works. The phone number is never sent. Withdraw: Settings > Confidentialité > "IA de Party".

USER CONTENT (1.2)
Terms before the first content, word filter, "Signaler" on events, polls, messages, profiles and photos, "Bloquer" on a profile (list: Settings > Confidentialité > "Personnes bloquées"). Contact: support@vendrediparty.ch.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Gérer mon compte" > type SUPPRIMER > "Supprimer définitivement mon compte".

PARTY GAMES (18+)
Some are drinking games for adults ("Alcool" category, optional sips setting). Nobody has to drink: water counts, 0 sips is alcohol-free. No gambling: no bets, purchases or prizes; Casino and Blackjack are not in this app.

WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Guests can play and request songs (3 games, 3 requests per party), then a verified phone number is asked (anti-abuse).

HOW TO TRY IT
1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Events > "Avec l'IA": describe an evening ("dinner at my place Friday 8 pm"), "J'accepte", answer, confirm, "Oui, continuer".
3. "Qui est invité": tap a "who sees it" option ("Envoyer pour validation").
4. Games: "Créer un quiz" (solo) or Méninges > "Faire chauffer".
5. Settings > Ton avis: write, "Envoyer".
