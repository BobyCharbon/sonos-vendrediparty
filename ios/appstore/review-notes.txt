ABOUT THE APP
Party is a free app made by friends in Switzerland (our group "Vendredi Party" lives inside it): organise events, play party games. French-speaking countries, 18+, in French.

SIGN-IN
Phone number + 6-digit SMS code, no password. Demo account "Apple-test" in Sign-in information (fixed code): tab "Se connecter" > "Se connecter" > number > code.

NATIVE FEATURES (4.2)
- Native tab bar; SwiftUI screens: Music, Settings, Groups, "Ton avis" (feedback), event AI assistant, "Pot commun" (shared expenses), announcement banner, offline screen.
- Push notifications, one switch per type; Live Activity "Ton titre arrive".
- Apple Wallet tickets, camera QR scanner, VisionKit receipt scanner, Calendar (date polls), optional Contacts (hashed numbers only), dictation (Speech), share extension, quick action, universal links, haptics.
- Pyramide game: 30 s to memorise your cards, in a native panel hidden from screenshots.

WEB CONTENT (2.5.2)
The Events and Games tabs are our own website (party.bobycharbon.ch) in a WKWebView: the same HTML/JS for every user, calling only the native features above. No code is downloaded to add or change native features; other sites open in Safari.

EVENTS (web, Events tab)
- Create an event (form or "Avec l'IA"), then "Qui est invité": who sees it (public, invitation, group), then invite.
- Invitation links, one per person or "Plusieurs" (several people). Each guest can create, copy and stop own links; sees counts only, never names.
- Not public: someone coming through a guest's link waits for the organiser ("À accepter").
- Date poll: guests vote dates, the organiser fixes one.
- "Ajouter à mon agenda": a personal calendar feed (subscribed once) follows the event and the answers.
- Carpool (organiser's option): participants offer or take seats. No money.
- Groups: "Inviter avec un lien" (reusable group link).
- Invited people sign in like everyone (SMS code, first and last name, nickname of 12 characters max) before answering.
- Event page updates live (arrivals, answers, carpool); tap a name to open the profile; each answer and date vote shows its time.
- Admins only: "Nouveau compte" notification; an announcement or maintenance message for everyone.

PAYMENTS (3.1.3(e))
Party sells nothing: no in-app purchase, no digital goods. An organiser may price a real-world event (seat, meal); participants pay the organiser outside the app (Twint, bank transfer, payment link), then tap "J'ai payé". "Pot commun" only records who owes what.

AI
"Avec l'IA", writing help in "Ton avis" and receipt reading in "Pot commun" send text/images to Anthropic (Claude) only after consent ("J'accepte"); all works without AI. Phone number never sent. Withdraw: Settings > Confidentialité > "IA de Party".

USER CONTENT (1.2)
Terms before the first content, word filter, "Signaler" on events, polls, messages, profiles, photos; "Bloquer" on a profile (Settings > Confidentialité > "Personnes bloquées"). Contact: support@vendrediparty.ch.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Gérer mon compte" > type SUPPRIMER > "Supprimer définitivement mon compte".

PARTY GAMES (18+)
Some are drinking games for adults ("Alcool" category, optional sips). Nobody has to drink: water counts, 0 sips is alcohol-free. No gambling: no bets, purchases or prizes; no Casino or Blackjack.

WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
3 games and 3 song requests per party, then a verified phone number (anti-abuse).

HOW TO TRY IT
1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Events > "Avec l'IA": describe an evening ("dinner at my place Friday 8 pm"), "J'accepte", answer, "Oui, continuer".
3. "Qui est invité": tap a "who sees it" option ("Envoyer pour validation").
4. Games: "Créer un quiz" (solo) or Méninges > "Faire chauffer".
5. Settings > Ton avis: write, "Envoyer".
