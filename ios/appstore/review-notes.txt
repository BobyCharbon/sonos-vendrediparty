ABOUT THE APP
VendrediParty ("Party") is a free app for a circle of friends in Switzerland: organise parties and events, and play party games together. Switzerland only, 18+, interface in French.

SIGN-IN
Phone number + 6-digit SMS code, no password. The demo account ("Apple-test") is in the Sign-in information fields; its code is fixed. Tab "Se connecter" > "Se connecter" > number > "Recevoir un code par SMS" > code.

NATIVE FEATURES (4.2)
- Native tab bar; Music, Settings, "Ton avis" (feedback), the event AI assistant, Groups and "Pot commun" (shared expenses) are SwiftUI screens.
- Push notifications, one switch per type (Settings > Notifications).
- Live Activity "Ton titre arrive" when your song request is about to play.
- Apple Wallet event tickets; camera QR scanner; VisionKit receipt scanner.
- Contacts (optional): members shown with your address-book names; only hashed numbers are compared.
- Dictation (Speech framework), share extension (song link to the party queue), quick action, universal links, haptics.

WEB CONTENT (2.5.2)
The Events and Games tabs come from our own website (party.bobycharbon.ch) in a WKWebView: the same HTML/JS as the website for all users. No executable code is downloaded to add or change native features. Other sites open in Safari.

PAYMENTS (3.1.3(e))
Party sells nothing: no in-app purchase, no digital goods, no commission. An organiser may set a price for a real-world event (a seat, a meal). Participants pay the organiser directly, outside the app (Twint, bank transfer, PayPal/Revolut link), then tap "J'ai payé"; the organiser confirms. "Pot commun" only records who owes what; money is paid outside the app.

AI
"Avec l'IA" (Events), the writing help in "Ton avis" and receipt reading in "Pot commun" send text/images to Anthropic (Claude) only after a consent sheet ("J'accepte"). Declining keeps everything working without AI. The phone number is never sent. Shown and withdrawn by every account in Settings > Confidentialité > "IA de Party" > "Retirer mon accord".

USER CONTENT (1.2)
Terms accepted before the first content. Automatic word filter. "Signaler" on events, polls, messages, profiles and photos; "Bloquer" on a member's profile; list in Settings > Confidentialité > "Personnes bloquées". Contact: support@vendrediparty.ch (in the Terms).

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Gérer mon compte" > type SUPPRIMER > "Supprimer définitivement mon compte".

PARTY GAMES (18+)
Some games are drinking games for adults (the "Alcool" category, and an optional sips setting in a few others). Nobody has to drink alcohol: a glass of water counts, and 0 sips is the alcohol-free version. No gambling: no bets, no purchases, no prizes. Casino and Blackjack are not offered in this app.

WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Guests can play and request songs without an account (3 games and 3 song requests per party). After that we ask for a verified phone number, to prevent abuse at a party (fake names, queue spam, ban evasion).

HOW TO TRY IT
1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Events tab > "Avec l'IA": describe an evening (e.g. "dinner at my place Friday 8 pm"), tap "J'accepte", answer, confirm the place, "Oui, continuer".
3. "Choisir qui est invité": tap a "who sees it" option > "Envoyer pour validation".
4. Games tab: "Créer un quiz" (works solo) or Méninges > "Faire chauffer".
5. Settings > Ton avis: write, "Envoyer", then "Modifier" under "Mes retours".

NOTES
- The demo account is a Guest+ member: its events wait for an admin's approval before others see them (meanwhile under Events > "Brouillons").
- Music is for a live party: guests scan the host's QR code or type its 4-digit code; with no party running, the Music tab shows that join screen.
- An Apple TV app (read-only: current game and music queue) shares this App Store record; it is not part of this submission.
