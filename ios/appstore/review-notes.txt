ABOUT
"Vendredi Party" (icon "Party !"): free app made by the Swiss association Vendredi Party, for friends and associations: real-world events, party games, music, group chats. French, 18+. No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchase. Full step-by-step guide attached (PDF).

SIGN-IN
Phone + 6-digit SMS code, no password. Demo account "Apple-test": number and code in the App Review Information sign-in fields (fixed code, no SMS). Last tab "Se connecter" > "Suisse +41" > number > code.

TRY IT
1. Events > "Soirée démo Vendredi Party" (invited, co-organiser): answer "Présent", order at the bar (Wallet card); "Gérer le bar".
2. Events > "Avec l'IA" or "+ Proposer" (new events wait for our approval).
3. Discussions > "Démo Apple": long-press a message > "Signaler".
4. Games > "Créer une salle" > Quiz (works alone), or Méninges > "Faire chauffer".

NATIVE (4.2)
Native tab bar; SwiftUI: Music, Settings, Discussions, Groups, Pronos, "Ton avis", AI assistant, "Pot commun", offline screen. Push (one switch per type), Live Activity, Wallet (tickets, bar card), QR scanner, VisionKit, Calendar, optional Contacts (hashed), dictation, share extension, universal links. Pyramide: cards in a native panel hidden from screenshots.

WEB CONTENT (2.5.2)
Events and Games tabs are our own site (party.bobycharbon.ch) in a WKWebView, same HTML/JS for all users, calling only the native features above. Other sites open in Safari. All is on in production, except "patates" (see PRONOS).

EVENTS
"+ Proposer" or "Avec l'IA", then "Qui est invité": public, invitation or groups. Links for one person or "Plusieurs"; guests create, copy and stop own links, see counts only. Not public: someone coming through a guest's link waits ("À accepter"). Date poll. "Ajouter à mon agenda": personal calendar feed. Carpool (no money). QR ticket + Wallet. Groups: "Inviter avec un lien". Everyone signs in (name, nickname of 12 characters max). The page updates live; tap a name to open the profile; each answer shows its time. Admins: "Nouveau compte" alert, announcement or maintenance message.

PAYMENTS (3.1.3(e))
Party never sells, holds or transfers money; no digital goods. Real-world events only: organiser's own Revolut/Twint/IBAN, ticketing link (Safari) or on site. "J'ai payé" is a note the organiser confirms. "Pot commun" only records who owes what.

PARTY BAR
Organiser's option, off by default; paid bar only on private events. Physical drinks, age checked in person by the barman; tobacco/drugs refused; no ranking or prompt to drink. Balance in a Wallet card, repaid to the organiser outside the app.

DISCUSSIONS (1.2)
Group chats and DMs between people sharing a group or event. Terms first, word filter, "Signaler" on messages, profiles, photos, events, polls, pronos. "Bloquer" hides that person's messages at once and notifies moderators. Reports handled within 24 h. Contact: support@vendrediparty.ch.

AI (5.1.2(i))
"Avec l'IA", "Ton avis" and receipt reading send data to Anthropic (Claude) only after "J'accepte". Withdraw: Settings > Confidentialité > "IA de Party".

GAMES (18+)
Drinking modes optional, water counts, never ranked by drinks. No real-money gambling, purchases or prizes; no Casino/Blackjack.

PRONOS (Simulated Gambling declared)
Settings > Autres apps > Pronos, or a group / event: friends guess an outcome for fun; points on the group ranking. "Patates": free play tokens, 100 per group or event each week (refilled to 100, never above), 1 to 20 per prono, shared among right answers (no odds, nothing created by the app). They cannot be bought, sold, given, transferred, converted or exchanged for anything; no prizes. Money words, betting sites and alcohol refused. Patates are switched on in production only after this version is approved. Demo: group "Démo Apple" > Pronos.

MUSIC
Needs a live party (host's QR or 4-digit code).

DELETION
Settings > "Gérer mon compte" > type SUPPRIMER > "Supprimer définitivement mon compte".
