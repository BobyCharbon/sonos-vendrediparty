# Braquage en miniature — surface brief

Mode: Operate / playable game. Code-led extension of Vendredi Party's established game surfaces. No static comp is binding. User chose the rules and explicitly authorized autonomous implementation and push to develop after answers.

## Confirmed request
Solo or cooperative shared loot for 2–8, accessible rounds of 3–5 minutes (four-minute limit selected), capture means prison with teammate rescue; solo costs time. Cartoon museum at night, red lasers and golden treasures. Three polished maps, top-down 3D, phone joystick + contextual action, keyboard on desktop. Existing game rooms, codes and mute control.

## Direction contract
- Structure: full playable stage, compact HUD with shared objective and timer, lobby map selection, separate large touch controls, minimap for navigation.
- Type: existing Bricolage Grotesque headings and system UI body text, clear action labels.
- Material: authored realtime cutaway museum geometry with columns, framed art, display cases, guards and masked robbers. Distinct layouts and palettes for three wings.
- Ground: navy night outside, blue marble / jade / sandstone inside; gold loot and action highlights, turquoise laser switch, green exit, pink prison. Catalogue card gets full shared theme tokens.
- Interaction: server-authoritative collisions, patrol vision with wall occlusion, visible exposure warning, contextual pickup/deposit/rescue/switch, shared success/failure and replay.

## Quality bar
On 320/390 portrait and short landscape, the local player, nearby loot, controls and contextual action must remain readable. No camera obstruction by full-height walls. Names must not overlap at spawn. Museum can crop in follow-camera mode; full minimap remains visible. All treasure locations and rescue/switch/exit coordinates must be reachable. UI and rules must explain dropped loot, solo time penalty, cooperative rescue and collective loss.

## Scope limits
Single-process host as existing games. No claims of actual iPhone or remote deployment validation without testing. No new team-competitive mode. Preserve unrelated local files.
