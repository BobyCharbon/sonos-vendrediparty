Vendredi Party — Guide for the App Review team

iOS app 1.0.0 · attachment to the App Review notes · updated 26 November 2026 (Guideline 1.4.3 follow-up)

B1. What Party is

Party ("Vendredi Party" on the App Store, "Party !" under the icon) is a free, non-commercial app published by Ludovic Trombert, an individual developer based in France. Party is for anyone who organises real-world events and game nights with friends. The in-app terms and privacy policy are up to date (publisher based in France).

What you can do with it:

  • Events — create an event (form or AI assistant), invite people with a link or a group, answer (Présent / Peut-être / Absent), vote on dates, share rides, get a QR ticket in Apple Wallet, pay the organiser outside the app for a real-world event.
  • Discussions — group chats and direct messages between people who know each other.
  • Games — party games played together on everyone's phone in a "game room", plus solo brain games. No drinking games: quizzes, card, word, party and 3D games with points only.
  • Music (no tab in the App Store app) — at a party at the host's place, a guest types the host's 6-digit code in Jeux › Code and adds songs to the host's queue (the host's own speakers and music account).
  • Settings — profile, notifications (one switch per type), groups, shared expenses ("Pot commun"), privacy, terms, account deletion.

The app is in French only, rated 18+, distributed in French-speaking countries. No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDK, no in-app purchase, nothing is sold.

Button labels below are given in French exactly as on screen, with an English explanation.

B4. Step-by-step tutorials

B4.1 Sign in

  1. Tap the last tab, Se connecter.
  2. Tap the row Se connecter ("With your number: events, games with an account, your stats").
  3. Keep Suisse +41 selected, type the demo number (with or without the leading 0).
  4. Tap Recevoir un code par SMS (Get a code by SMS).
  5. Type the fixed 6-digit code: the app signs in automatically at the 6th digit (or tap Se connecter).
  6. The last tab is now Réglages (Settings) with the profile at the top, and the Discussions tab appears.

A new account (not the demo one) then fills in "Faisons connaissance": first name, last name, nickname (12 characters max); the photo is optional. Why a last name: Party is for real-world events, often at someone's home; a full name lets Party's admins know who an account belongs to when they approve new accounts or handle a report. The first and last name are private: the form says "🔒 Ton prénom et ton nom ne sont visibles que par toi et les admins" (the person and Party's admins only); other members see the nickname and photo, unless the person turns on Afficher mon nom et prénom in Réglages › Mon profil (off by default).

B4.2 Browse events

Tab Événements:

  • Pills: À venir (upcoming), Sondages (date polls), Brouillons (drafts and proposals waiting for approval), Passés (past), Filtres (categories, tags).
  • Top right: magnifier (search) and bell (activity).
  • Bottom right: ✨ Avec l'IA (AI assistant) and + Proposer (create with a form).
  • Tap a card to open the event page. Its sections, top to bottom: title, date, place (tap: directions in Maps), "À propos", Ajouter à mon agenda, your answer, the event's polls (right under the answer), your ticket, the "Invités" card, carpool, details, participants, "Qui amène quoi" (who brings what), discussion, Signaler cet événement, and for organisers the ZONE ORGANISATEUR. The page updates live (answers, arrivals, carpool).
  • Tap any name to open that person's profile (with Écrire, Bloquer, Signaler le profil).

B4.3 Create an event with the form

  1. Événements › + Proposer.
  2. Choose Simple or Avancé (advanced: tags, places, price, date poll, polls, who brings what, forum…).
  3. Fill in Titre, Description, Quand (date), Où (place search or text).
  4. To publish at once: in Qui › "Qui est invité ?", tap Sur invitation, then tick Démo Apple · 2, then Publier. The form opens on "Publique + lien": a public event, or an invitation-only event with no group, shows Envoyer pour validation instead (our moderators check it first, 1.2). Enregistrer en brouillon keeps it as a draft.
  5. The first time you publish content, the Terms of use sheet appears: accept to continue.
  6. An event sent for approval waits in Brouillons with the badge "En attente de validation". Once a moderator approves it, it is online. We approve demo-account proposals quickly during the review.

B4.4 Create an event with the AI assistant ("Créer avec l'IA")

  1. Événements › ✨ Avec l'IA: a native screen opens ("Créer avec l'IA", steps 1 La soirée · 2 Qui est invité · 3 C'est en ligne).
  2. Décris ton événement: type or dictate (microphone, Apple dictation) e.g. "Raclette chez moi vendredi 20 h", optionally attach a photo or screenshot of a poster.
  3. Envoyer. The first time, a consent sheet explains what is sent to Anthropic (Claude): J'accepte or Pas maintenant (then the form remains available).
  4. "L'IA prépare ton événement…" (20–40 s). If something is missing, "Il me manque quelques infos": answer below. A place must be confirmed on the map ("Touche le bon").
  5. Summary "C'est juste ?" › Oui, continuer (or Corriger).
  6. Step 2 "Qui est invité" (web page) offers three choices: 🌍 Public + lien public, 👥 Des groupes and ✉️ Sur invitation seulement. Tap 👥 Des groupes, tick "Démo Apple", then "Publier pour …": the event is published at once. For the demo account, a public event or an invitation-only event with no group is sent for approval (moderators check it first). The AI never chooses who is invited or who sees the event, never turns on tickets, carpool or group announcements, and never chooses a payment method. The AI receives your nickname, the names of your groups, categories and tags, and people already chosen under an alias (P1, P2…), never the list of members.
  7. Step 3: the event page opens. To change it later, use Modifier (the form).

B4.5 Invite people

On an online event you organise (e.g. one you create), card Invités › + Inviter:

  • Une personne (one person) or Plusieurs (several people: unlimited by default, − / + to set places), optional link name ("Nom du lien"), then Partager le lien… (iOS share sheet: Messages, WhatsApp…) or copy it.
  • The person opens the link, signs in with their phone like everyone, and answers.
  • Each guest has the same "Invités" card with counts only (never names) and can create, copy and stop their own links. On a non-public event, someone who comes through a guest's link waits for the organiser: section À accepter in the organiser's "Invités" card.
  • Groups: Réglages › Groupes › a group › Inviter avec un lien (reusable group link).
  • Friends: Réglages › Mes amis dans Party (optional; Contacts access is asked only after an explanation, and limited access is respected). It lists your phone contacts already on Party (matched with a hash of each number made on the iPhone; names never leave it), and contacts not yet on Party: each has its own button that opens Apple's Messages composer with your personal link, which you read and send yourself, one contact at a time. No select-all; Party sends nothing.
  • Attendance by hand: organisers can add people to the attendance by hand, members or people without an account (e.g. "Théo, Léa's cousin"), to count them; people without an account are never notified and their names are never shown to guests, who only see numbers; names pass the same filter.

B4.6 Answer an event

On an event page: Présent (going), Peut-être (maybe), Absent (not going). Each answer shows its time. Absent removes you from the carpool. The event's polls sit right under the answer; on an event with a fixed date, voting in them is open once you answered Présent or Peut-être.

B4.7 Vote on a date poll

On an event whose date is still a poll (none is prepared in the demo data) › for each date Oui / Si besoin / Non › Valider mes dates. The organiser later taps Fixer cette date: "Oui" and "Si besoin" become "Présent".

B4.8 Add to calendar

On an event: Ajouter à mon agenda. The first time, iOS asks to subscribe to a personal calendar "Vendredi Party" (a private feed for this account); afterwards the event follows any change on its own. In a date poll, the native app can also put the proposed dates in the iPhone calendar (permission asked at that moment) and remove the unused ones once the date is fixed.

B4.9 Carpool

On an event where the organiser turned it on (option off by default; off on the demo event), card Covoiturage: 🚗 Je conduis (offer seats, optional departure town) or 🙋 Je cherche une place, then Monter / Prendre. Only participants and organisers take part. No money, no cost sharing.

B4.10 QR ticket and Apple Wallet

On an event with the option "Billets QR" (on for the demo event: answer Présent, then hand yourself the ticket with Remettre à tous les présents in the organiser card Billets QR of the event page), once your ticket is handed to you, card Ton billet › Afficher ›: the QR code to show at the door, and Apple's badge Ajouter à l'app Cartes Apple (native PassKit sheet). Organisers hand a ticket with Remettre le billet (one guest) or Remettre à tous les présents (card Billets QR, also on the entry screen), or it arrives automatically when they confirm a payment; they check tickets at the door with 📷 Scanner les billets (ZONE ORGANISATEUR, Apple's native QR reader). A ticket never exists unless the organiser turned tickets on.

B4.11 Paying for a real-world event (outside the app)

An organiser may set a price per person for a real-world event (a dinner, a seat, an activity) and choose how people pay:

  • On me paie (pay me): the organiser's own payment link (e.g. Revolut), Twint number or IBAN, shown to participants;
  • Paiement en ligne: a ticketing / payment page of a third party (https link);
  • Sur place: cash or card on the day — nothing to do in the app.

Guest side, on a priced event after Présent (the demo event is free): Payer [organiser] · 10 CHF opens the organiser's details; a payment link opens outside the app (Safari or the provider's app). Back in Party, J'ai payé is only a note to the organiser. With On me paie, the organiser confirms it: Reçu ✓ / Pas reçu. With Paiement en ligne, the organiser simply sees who noted they paid (the third-party site is not theirs to check). While "Paiement ouvert" is off, guests only see "paiement bientôt possible".

Party never sells anything, never processes, holds or transfers money, and offers no digital goods: these are payments between real people for real-world goods and services consumed outside the app (guideline 3.1.3(e)). No in-app purchase is involved.

B4.12 Discussions (group chats, direct messages, message requests)

Tab Discussions (signed-in accounts):

  1. Open Démo Apple: group conversation with a few text messages; text, photos and voice messages can be sent, with replies, reactions and mentions.
  2. The first message you send asks you to accept the Terms of use.
  3. Long-press a message › Répondre, Copier, Signaler (report: choose a reason, optional comment, optional box "Joindre les 10 messages précédents"; a reported photo or voice message is copied for our moderators).
  4. Who can write to whom (decided by the database): people with a real link (a shared group other than "Membres Vendredi Party", a shared private event, or the organiser of an event you answered) write directly. Anyone else can only send a message request: one text message, no photo, voice, link or phone number, nothing more until accepted.
  5. Demandes (top of the list): each request shows the sender's mini-profile and message with Accepter, Supprimer, Bloquer, Signaler. The sender is never told of a refusal. Settings › Confidentialité › Demandes de message: "Tout membre" (default) or "Personne". Limits: 10 new requests a day, 30 pending.
  6. Nouveau message (pink button): suggestions come only from real links (your groups, people met at an event in the last 60 days, your phone contacts already on Party if you allow Contacts); search by first name, last name or nickname, never by phone number. Choosing 2 or more people creates a private group (name, emoji, colour, invitations to accept), which our administrators never read; it is moderated through reports, like direct messages.
  7. Mini-profile: tap any name or photo (chats, guest lists, groups…) › Envoyer un message (disabled with the reason when not allowed), "Déjà vu N fois" (past parties attended together), upcoming events in common, Bloquer · Signaler.
  8. Blocking: messages from the blocked person disappear at once (groups, direct messages, event discussions), removes their requests, notifies our moderators, and stops them writing to you, mentioning, inviting you or seeing your profile; they are not told. Unblock: Réglages › Confidentialité › Personnes bloquées.
  9. Read receipts ("Vu") in direct chats only if both people keep Confirmations de lecture on; "écrit…" is a live signal, never stored. Neither appears in a request or with a blocked person.

Each event can also have its own Discussion (organiser's option "Forum de l'événement"), with the same rules.

B4.13 Groups

Réglages › Groupes: your groups, Nouveau groupe, members, Inviter avec un lien (QR, copy, share), "Montrer mon numéro dans ce groupe" (hidden by default), leave a group. On a group's page, Signaler le groupe reports its name (and Signaler la photo du groupe its photo) to our moderators. Group admins can set roles (co-admin, organiser) and who may invite, create events, write or pin; optional approval of new members.

Event guests in a group (organiser option, off by default). In step 2 "Qui est invité" or in the event's guest card ("Régler"), the organiser can pick one group they manage and belong to, or "Nouveau groupe" (name filtered, terms required); "Membres Vendredi Party" can never be chosen. Each guest then sees, right under the answer buttons, a large unticked box "Rejoindre aussi le groupe « … » de …" that explains what members will see (nickname, name, photo; the phone number stays hidden), that they can write in the group chat and invite them to future events, and that they can leave at any time. Only a guest who ticks the box joins (whatever the answer); the answer never depends on the box. A guest who answered without ticking keeps a "Rejoindre le groupe" button on the event page (no reminder, no notification). After joining, a personal notification "Tu es dans le groupe X" (own switch in Réglages › Notifications) and a banner in the group chat with Quitter; group members are not notified. Someone who left is never put back without their own action; blocked people are never added. Day after the event, the organiser may see "🎉 Garder cette bande ?": it only sends invitations to the group, once per event, which each person accepts or declines. No reward of any kind is attached to joining.

B4.14 Games

Tab Jeux:

  • Salle de jeux (game room): Créer une salle (or type a friend's 4-digit code in Code › Rejoindre). Others join with the QR or the code. In the room, everyone votes for the next game; the host taps a game to launch it, and 🎮 Changer de jeu brings everyone back to the room. Quiz can be played alone; most other games need 2+ phones.
  • 🧠 Jeux solo › Méninges › Faire chauffer: daily brain warm-up (speed, memory, logic), 3 lives.
  • Each game has a ? with its rules.
  • No drinking games (since the 1.4.3 review, 25.11). Removed from the App Store app, permanently for this version: every game linked to drinking by its origin, rules or texts (Pyramide, Je n'ai jamais, Qui de nous ?, Roulette, Beer pong, Biskit, Purify Test), every drink option of the other games, the "Hot" / "Mix" question modes and the "WTF" theme of Pour ou contre. They are not renamed or turned into points: they are simply absent. The remaining games are Quiz, Qui est le plus… ?, L'Intrus, Président, Pour ou contre, Le Divan, Tu me connais ?, Le Masque, Le Reflet, Qui es-tu vraiment ?, the 3D games and Méninges; their questions and words never mention alcohol, drunkenness or drugs. Our server refuses the removed games to the App Store app at creation, by code, by QR, in a game room and when reading a game.
  • There is no gambling: no bets, no money, no prizes, no purchases. Casino and Blackjack are hidden and refused by our server in the App Store app.
  • In the App Store app, games stay unlimited without an account; an account (phone number) is needed only for events, groups and chats.
  • Players: before joining or creating a game, each player ticks "J'ai 18 ans ou plus et j'accepte les conditions d'utilisation" (the words "conditions d'utilisation" open the terms). Our server refuses any text shown to the table (first name, answer, proposal) from a player who has not ticked it. First names and written answers pass the word filter, including a first name changed during a game. Any player can leave a game at any time; the host can remove and ban a player; written answers can be reported ("Signaler") and are then hidden for the whole table.
  • Index of games (4.7.4): all games are first-party, written and hosted by the developer; there are no third-party mini apps or games. Universal link to the games: https://party.bobycharbon.ch/?tab=games (opens the Jeux tab of the app). Games offered in the App Store app: Quiz, Qui est le plus… ?, L'Intrus, Président, Pour ou contre, Le Divan, Tu me connais ?, Le Masque, Le Reflet, Qui es-tu vraiment ?, the 3D games Néon Knockout, L’île qui s’effondre and Braquage en miniature (13 games), and Méninges (solo).

B4.15 Music

There is no Music tab in the App Store app. At a party at the host's place (the host's speakers and music account), a guest opens Jeux, types the host's 6-digit party code in Code (4 digits = a game) or scans the host's QR: the native Music screen opens. Search a song ("Titre, artiste ou lien") and add it to the shared queue. Optional Live Activity for your song: while one of your songs is queued, a countdown "Ton titre arrive" can show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; it ends by itself once the song has played, and it can be turned off in the iPhone's Settings › Party (Live Activities). Without a running party there is nothing to join: Music needs a host playing on Sonos at home, so it cannot be opened without a party running at the host's place. Search results are credited to Spotify ("Résultats Spotify", each song opens in Spotify); song suggestions never require an account. The share extension ("Party" in the share sheet) does not touch Music in this app: it only offers "Créer l'événement" from a shared link (the AI assistant drafts the event, you review it before publishing).

B4.16 Shared expenses ("Pot commun")

Réglages › Pot commun: shared expense lists between friends (who paid, who owes what); names, titles and notes pass the app's word filter. A pot is joined with its link, or automatically through an event: when the organiser of an event chose a pot for it, everyone who answers Présent is added to that pot, and so is anyone the organiser marks Présent; anyone can leave it at any time in Réglages › Pot commun › Quitter ce pot commun. Members of a pot see each member's name; a member's payment details (IBAN, Revolut, PayPal, Twint) stay hidden in that pot until the member chooses Montrer mes moyens de paiement dans ce pot; this is stated in the privacy policy. Adding an expense or a receipt photo requires accepting the Terms of use, and each expense and receipt photo has Signaler (report). Receipts can be scanned (VisionKit; optional AI reading with consent). It only records amounts; repayments happen outside the app: "Rembourser" only shows the friend's own IBAN, Revolut, PayPal or Twint (opens Safari or copies it). No money goes through Party and nothing digital is unlocked.

B4.17 Feedback ("Ton avis")

Réglages › Ton avis (shown to members whose role includes it; a brand-new account may not have it): send an idea or a bug report to the Party team (the publisher); optional AI writing help (consent required).

B4.18 Notifications

Réglages › Notifications: one switch per type, grouped as Général, Jeux, Ton avis, Événements (new event, reminders, event discussions…), Discussions des groupes, Amis, Comptes et modération, Quand j'organise. Music notification settings appear only for the host of a music party; a guest's switches are on the Music screen. The moon icon (top right of Réglages) pauses notifications for a while. Each event page has 🔔 Sourdine (mute). The app works without notifications. iOS permission is asked only when you turn them on (e.g. the banner "Ne rate rien de cet événement" on an event page).

B4.19 Privacy, AI consent, blocked people, terms

Réglages › Confidentialité: "Noms de mes contacts" (optional), Personnes bloquées, Conditions d'utilisation (terms, contact), the IA de Party consent (withdraw or give again), then the full privacy policy.

B4.20 Delete the account

Réglages › Gérer mon compte › type SUPPRIMER › Supprimer définitivement mon compte. It deletes the profile, photos, answers, votes, messages, the events the person created, game stats, AI consents and AI usage log. Se déconnecter (sign out) is just below.

B5. Content that is loaded from our server

Honestly stated: the Events and Games tabs (and a few settings pages for admins) are our own website, https://party.bobycharbon.ch, displayed in a WKWebView, maintained by us. Our server recognises the App Store app (its User-Agent) and always gives every user of this app the same dedicated version: no drinking game or drink option, no party bar, no Casino / Blackjack, no Hot / Mix questions, no Music tab. Our public website and other platforms are separate; the app never opens their games.

  • What comes from the server: the whole Events module (lists, event pages, invitations, date polls, tickets, payments, carpool, event discussions, activity), all multiplayer games and Méninges, the admin "Annonce et maintenance" page.
  • What is native in the binary: tab bar, Music screen (guest and host, opened from Jeux › Code), Settings and all its pages, Discussions, Groups, "Ton avis", the AI event assistant, "Pot commun", offline screen, announcement banner, QR scanner, Wallet, Calendar, Contacts, share extension, push notifications.
  • The page can only call a fixed set of native features exposed by the app (window.PartyNative: tabs, push, Wallet, calendar, contacts names, QR scan, AI screen, event chat, announcement, haptics). Server content never adds or changes native features, and no executable code is downloaded into the binary (2.5.2).
  • Only our domain, our authentication provider and the host's music sign-in open inside the app; every other link opens in Safari (no unrestricted web browsing).
  • Because the Events and Games tabs are web content, they can be improved without a new binary; any major new feature delivered this way is described in these notes before it is turned on in production (2.3.1(a)). Pronos, Agenda, the companion and Souvenirs (event photos) are not available in this app, and the removed games stay removed for version 1.0.0. They would only come in a future app version submitted for review and approved, never for this version.

B6. User-generated content and safety (1.2)

  • Who can post: only signed-in people with a verified phone number (answers and votes also need a complete profile: first name, last name, nickname).
  • Terms of use must be accepted before the first content (message, event, group, nickname…); they forbid harassment, hate, sexual or violent content, illegal content, spam; zero tolerance. Users must be 18 or older.
  • Word filter on messages, nicknames, names, group names, places and texts shown in games.
  • Signaler (report) on every message, profile, profile photo, event and poll, and on the players' written answers shown to the table in Le Masque, Tu me connais ? and the dilemmas proposed in Pour ou contre (hidden at once for everyone, logged for moderators), on group names and photos (Signaler le groupe), and on "Pot commun" expenses and receipt photos. In every game the host can remove or ban a player.
  • Bloquer (block) from a profile, a mini-profile, a direct chat or a message request; messages from a blocked person disappear at once and the block is reported to moderators.
  • Message requests: people without a real link can only send one text message, which the recipient accepts or not; "Demandes de message: Personne" turns them off. Direct messages and private groups are never read by our admins; reported content (with its photo or voice message) is copied for moderators.
  • Moderation: reports are read and handled by the publisher within 24 hours, with a copy of the content; the publisher hides content (the author is told why and how to appeal), warns, suspends or bans accounts.
  • Contact published in the app (Settings › Confidentialité › Conditions d'utilisation) and on the support page: support@vendrediparty.ch.
